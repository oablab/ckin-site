Open it where you are: three changes in ckin 1.0.1 and 1.0.2

November 2026 · ckin dev notes #2

These two versions change three things you meet right after opening the app: where the map starts, where your check-in posts open, and what is near the place you plan to go.

TL;DR
  • Centered on you: open the app and the map is centered on where you are, with the check-in shops around you in view.
  • Posts open in Threads: when you look at an earlier check-in, ckin switches to the Threads app and opens that post.
  • Share from Google Maps: find a place in Google Maps, share it to ckin, then open ckin to see the check-in shops near that place.
  • All three are in ckin for iOS 1.0.1 and 1.0.2. Update to the latest version to get them.

The map opens where you are

On ckin a check-in only counts if you were there, so the most useful place for the map to start is where you are standing.

Now the map opens centered on your current location, with the check-in shops nearby already on screen. Red pins are shops you have not eaten at yet; a green ✓ means you have checked in there.

On the way home, or while you wait for a friend, one look tells you which shop is a short walk away.

Past check-ins open in Threads

Every check-in is a post on your own Threads.

Now, when you look at an earlier check-in in ckin, one tap switches to the Threads app on your phone and opens that post. The post is already on your account, so you read it in Threads the way you read any other post.

Share a place from Google Maps

Many people plan where to go in Google Maps, and before heading out it helps to know what is worth eating near there.

Now you can find the place in Google Maps, tap Share, and pick ckin in the share sheet. Open ckin and the map is centered on that place, with the check-in shops around it.

The place you share is used only on your phone, to find its coordinates.

The map starts where you are,
or where you are about to go.

None of these changes is big. They share one aim: a little less scrolling around the map, and a few more steps into the shop.
